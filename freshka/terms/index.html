Dates and advice

The shelf lives the app shows are estimates. Before eating something, check the date on its pack, how it looks and how it smells. The app does not judge whether a particular food is safe: what to eat is always your decision.

Photo recognition

Names, amounts and dates found in a photo are guessed by an AI model and may be wrong. Check them before adding.

Shared fridge and shared catalogue

Anyone you invite into a fridge sees everything in it and can change items just like you. Invite people you trust.

Photos and names you add to the shared barcode catalogue are seen by every user. Do not add anything personal, or anything you do not have the rights to.
